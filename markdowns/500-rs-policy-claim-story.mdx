A real claim story from Share India Insurance Brokers

**When an ordinary morning turned into a medical emergency**

For the Sharma family in Pune, healthcare insurance had always felt like an optional expense. With a tight monthly budget and school fees to manage, they chose a modest ₹500-per-month health insurance policy, just enough to feel protected.

In July, that small decision proved life-changing.

Mr. Sharma suffered a sudden cardiac emergency and required immediate hospitalization and angioplasty. Within hours, the family faced a reality many Indian households fear: a medical bill exceeding ₹4 lakh—far beyond their savings.

What could have become long-term debt instead became a story of financial survival—because the right policy was already in place.

## The event in short — what happened

- **Sudden chest pain** led to emergency admission in a network hospital.
- **Doctors advised urgent angioplasty** within the golden hour.
- **Estimated treatment cost:** ₹3.8–₹4.2 lakh.
- **Family savings available:** less than ₹70,000.
- **Without insurance,** the family would likely have relied on loans or asset liquidation.

## How Share India Insurance Brokers turned panic into protection

### (our role, step-by-step)

1. **Immediate policy validation** We quickly verified policy status, coverage limits, waiting-period compliance, and hospital network eligibility—ensuring no procedural delay during a life-saving emergency.
2. **Cashless approval coordination** Our team worked directly with the insurer and hospital TPA to secure cashless authorization before surgery, removing the burden of arranging lakhs in advance.
3. **Documentation & medical justification** We guided the hospital desk and family on submitting correct medical notes, diagnostics, and treatment rationale—preventing common claim objections.
4. **Claim settlement support** The insurer approved the majority of expenses under hospitalization, procedure cost, ICU, and medicines—resulting in minimal out-of-pocket expense for the family.
5. **Financial recovery planning** Post-treatment, we advised the family on increasing coverage and adding critical illness protection to safeguard future risks.

## What this story teaches every household

- **Even low-premium health insurance** can prevent financial collapse.
- **Cashless hospitalization matters** more than reimbursement during emergencies.
- **Correct documentation** often decides claim approval vs. rejection.
- **A proactive broker** ensures speed, clarity, and full utilization of benefits.

## Final note

For the Sharma family, ₹500 per month wasn’t an expense—it was the difference between recovery and lifelong debt.
