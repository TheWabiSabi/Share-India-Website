**Don’t Wait for a Medical Emergency to Think About Health Insurance**

Most people plan to buy health insurance…

- “next month,”
- “after appraisal,”
- “when things settle down.”

But medical emergencies don’t wait for the right time.

A recent case reminded us of this reality.
A healthy individual in his early 30s faced sudden hospitalization due to an unexpected illness.
Without insurance, the entire treatment cost had to be arranged overnight — through savings, loans, and help from relatives.
The financial recovery took far longer than the medical one.

Health insurance, taken earlier, would have meant:

- Cashless treatment instead of emergency borrowing
- Protection of personal savings
- Peace of mind for the entire family

The best time to buy health insurance isn’t after diagnosis.
It’s when you’re still healthy enough to choose freely.
Because preparation today prevents regret tomorrow.
